# F.E.T.S.U. presents Srijan 2026 General Info
- **About Srijan:** In the heart of Kolkata, where passion ignites, Srijan flourishes — an annual tribute to creativity and excellence, cradled by Jadavpur. From its inception in 2007 as a humble spark, it has flourished into a spectacular festival of technology and management. In this four-day spectacle, Srijan transcends from being merely a festival; it becomes a chorus of the most brilliant minds, echoing through the halls of Jadavpur's heritage. Join us at Jadavpur University to engage in 50+ events ranging from Coding Competitions, B-Plan competitions, Case Studies, Equity Research Events, Robotics Competitions, Web Design, Rap Battles, and many more to have an experience of a lifetime.
- **About Jadavpur University:** Founding members of National Council of Bengal, in 1906 set the goal "To achieve self reliance through empowerment of Youth by imparting Best of Global Knowledge", and Jadavpur University, founded on 24th December 1955, continues to do the same today. The university's commitment to research, innovation, community engagement, a legendary network of alumnus, and its unwavering contribution to imparting knowledge and refining the taste of culture and technology has marked its distinguished reputation not only in the Indian subcontinent but also at an international level.
- **Dates:** March 9th - 12th, 2026.

# Guide: How to Sign Up
- **Link:** /signup
- **Description:** Step-by-step guide to create your account for Srijan 2026.
- **Steps:**
  1. Visit the official website: srijanju.in
  2. Click on the **"Login"** button at the top right.
  3. Sign up using your Google Account or Email.
  4. Complete your profile by entering your College Name, Department, and Phone Number.
  5. Once done, you will be navigated to the dashboard.

# Guide: How to Participate in Events
- **Description:** Instructions for registering for specific events after logging in.
- **Link:** /events
- **Steps:**
  1. Ensure you are logged in to the portal.
  2. Go to the **"Events"** tab and browse the categories (Coding, Robotics, Gaming, etc.).
  3. Click on the event card you are interested in (e.g., "Pass the Baton").
  4. Click the **"Register"** button on the event page.
  5. **For Team Events:**
     - One member creates the team -> This generates a **Team Code**.
     - Share this code with your teammates.
     - Teammates enter the code in their portal to join the team.
  6. **For Individual Events:** Simply click Register to confirm your slot.

# Guide: How to Order Official Srijan Merchandise
- **Link:** /merchandise
- **Description:** Step-by-step guide to purchase the official Srijan 2026 T-shirt.
- **Steps:**
  1. Ensure you are logged in to the portal.
  2. Navigate to the **"Merchandise"** page from the website menu or visit **/merchandise**.
  3. Select your preferred **T-shirt Size** from the available options (XS – XXXXL).
  4. Choose your preferred **Color** (White or Black).
  5. Select whether you want **Custom Text** printed on the back of the T-shirt.
     - If **Yes**, enter the text you want printed on the back.
     - If **No**, proceed without entering any text.
  6. Click the **"Buy Now"** button.
  7. Complete the payment using one of the available payment methods:
     - UPI
     - Debit/Credit Card
     - Net Banking
  8. After successful payment, your **merchandise order will appear on your Dashboard** under your purchases.
